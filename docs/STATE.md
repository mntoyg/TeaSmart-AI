## Remaining before the demo

Complete the phone checklist in `docs/DEMO.md`: use a secure context, verify
the camera and online scan, install the PWA, prepare its offline assets, scan
in airplane mode, reconnect, and confirm queued results sync without duplicate
history entries. LAN HTTP alone cannot provide camera or service-worker APIs;
the runbook documents a local Chrome secure-origin option and an HTTPS tunnel.

The frontend's offline result is intentionally `classifier_only`: it does not
include server-side lesion analysis, burn judgement or severity. The result
card must preserve that label. The 50-image parity gate validates image
preprocessing and classifier output; it does not make the full server and
offline analyses equivalent.

## Model and evidence limits

- The shipped browser model is fp32 ONNX at 16.8 MB. The int8 graph is not used
  because its probability delta exceeded the contract bound.
- The held-out score is not a field-accuracy estimate: the split is random by
  class, not grouped by photography session, and no corpus photograph comes
  from Singha Park.
- Burn specificity is 97.18% on 1,030 confirmed-disease images. Burn
  sensitivity cannot be measured because no labelled real burn photographs
  exist; do not quote a sensitivity value.
- Thirty-two cross-source label disagreements are excluded rather than guessed.
  Kaggle and gated HuggingFace sources remain disabled pending credentials;
  neither is required for this demo.
